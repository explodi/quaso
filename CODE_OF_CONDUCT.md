# Code of conduct

We want Quaso's community to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, origin, language,
identity or background. Translators, game developers and operators all take part, often in a
language that isn't their first.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback on work graciously.
- Respect people's time and privacy.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers, through the contact listed on the project's
repository. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, and warn or ban
participants who break this code, in any of the project's spaces.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version
2.1.
